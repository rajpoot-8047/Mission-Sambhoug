# 🎲 Physics Dice Animator Agent

## Mission
Eliminate linear, artificial dice motions and deliver museum-grade, physics-inspired dice rolling:
- **Toss Mechanics**: Rapid initial vertical toss with continuous angular tumbling across X, Y, and Z axes.
- **Dynamic Rebound**: Parabolic gravity curve with secondary rebound bounce and dampened settling wobble.
- **Dynamic Contact Shadows**: Shadow radius expands and softens during high flight; contracts and sharpens upon tabletop contact.
- **Zero Snapping**: Face alignment rotates smoothly to target value upon resting with seamless quaternions or trigonometric curves.
- **Synchronized Audio**: Multi-stage sound impact triggered at the moment of peak velocity and tabletop bounces.
